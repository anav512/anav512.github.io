Research Jan – May 2025

Assistive Mobile Robot (MoBot)

Undergraduate Research Assistant · Human Centered Autonomy Lab, UIUC

Description & Scope

This project has three major objectives. The first is the modification of a stable commercially available walker by integrating a motorized wheel system, tactile or force-sensitive handles, and sensors. The next objective is to develop a control system that can translate user intent into robotic action. The system is expected to interpret physical movements and understand natural language commands in order to assist the user safely and effectively. Finally, the research group hopes to evaluate the system with real elderly users.
