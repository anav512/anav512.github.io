About

I am a senior majoring in Systems Engineering & Design at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, with an interest in the control and monitoring of complex systems. Relevant coursework includes control theory, engineering design & validation, signal processing, and robot control.

This interest has led me to work with the Monolithic Systems Lab at UIUC, where I am applying engineering design principles to assistive technology research, designing a soft robotic shower system for individuals with limited mobility.

In addition, I have also explored the field of power and sustainable energy through my involvement in the Illini Solar Car Team and an independent study on power-grid optimization.
